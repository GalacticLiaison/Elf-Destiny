# Aeluran Matronates at each bookmark (867, 1066, 1178): which Matron holds which Matronate, and the region it covers.
# Each bookmark has its own copy of every Matron (_1066 / _1178); a Matronate is vacated the day after its holder's
# history death, so the office never passes to a family heir between bookmarks.
# The region is restated with every new holder in case vacating the title drops it.
# Every holder takes the Order government: a region takes its domicile type from its holder's government when bound,
# and the engine cannot hand a region on without one. The Order government carries the Matron's Hall.
