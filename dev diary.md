# Dev Diary: The Dark Elves Arrive

Greetings, elves and elf-enjoyers. It has been a long time coming, and today we finally get to pull back the curtain on the largest addition to Elf Destiny yet. A second elven people steps out of legend and into the game: the Dark Elves. This diary walks through what ships in the release, what it looks like, and a peek at what is still on the workbench.

Grab a drink. There is a lot to cover.

<IMAGE OF a dark elf ruler on the character screen, purple skin and glowing eyes, as a splash/banner>

## Designing a Dark Elf

The Ruler Designer is where most of you will meet the Dark Elves for the first time. When you open it up you will find a new race track sitting alongside the elves you already know. Human, Elf, and now Dark Elf each get their own path.

<IMAGE OF the Ruler Designer race selection showing Human / Elf / Dark Elf categories>

For now the dark elf tiers share their names with the standard elven tiers. That is a placeholder we may revisit, so do not be surprised if the labels change in a later build once we settle on the right flavor for each rung of the ladder.

The bigger news is the new Elven appearance tab. Dark Elves come in three signature skin tones, and you can dial in exactly the look you want.

<IMAGE OF the appearance tab cycling through the three dark elf skin tones>

And because no dark elf court is complete without a little ceremony etched into the skin, we have added tattoos. Mark your ruler and your dynasty with them and wear your allegiances openly.

<GIF OF applying different tattoo patterns to a dark elf portrait>

## The Divine Spark Judges You

The first expedition story line has been updated! Now when you first touch the Sigil of the Realm Lord you will be judged by the Divine Spark based on your traits and lifestyle. It will decide if your character most resonates with normal or dark elves and transform you into that selection.

<IMAGE OF the "You Are Known" judgment event where the Spark delivers its one-word verdict>

You are not entirely without a say. You can accept the verdict, or you can defy it and claim the other path through force of will.
Upon choosing Dark Elf you will be able to select a skin and hair color and your whole adventure party will be transformed along side you!

<IMAGE OF the skin/hair choice step of the transformation>

Ive also added the ability to choose a pre-existing elf culture at this point too, for those dont want to start with a blank slate culture.

## New Cultures

The Dark Elves are not one people but two: 
Dökkálfar: Grey elves of the cold north that follow the teachings of the Dark Valar Melkor
Drow: Matriarchal elves of the shadowed jungles that worship the Spider Goddess Lloth and Snake God Typhos

Each Dark Elf culture comes with a unique dark Elf tradition that reflects the way they rule.
Dark Elves also have their own variant of "The Old Ways" tradition.


<IMAGE OF the culture screen for one of the new dark elf cultures showing its traditions>

## New Bloodline Dynasties

Four new prominent Dark Elven families have been added  

## New Kingdoms 

The Dark Elves are not squatters in the margins. They hold real seats of power, and each has been given its own name and its own color so you can spot them at a glance.

The tyrants of the north rule Svartalfaheim from a wyvern throne. In the deep jungles the serpent queen holds Valusia. And the spider matriarchs weave their webs from Menzoberranzan.

<IMAGE OF the political map highlighting the three dark elf realms in their distinct colors>



## Ancient Houses and Their Blood

This is the part we are most excited about. The Dark Elves arrive with four ancient dynasties, each thousands of years old, each with its own patron, its own sigil, and a bloodline trait that leaves a visible mark on every member of the line.

- The Drenmyre wear the wyvern and rule the north through dread. Their eyes turn to black.
- The Rynvela are raven-marked necromancers who commune with the dead. Their lips turn black.
- The Myrvessa are the spider matriarchs, favored of the goddess of the deep. Their hair runs blood red.
- The Syldranis chase reckless evolution in the name of a serpent god. Their eyes burn vibrant green.

<IMAGE OF a side-by-side of the four bloodline founders showing each house's signature mark>

These are not faceless placeholders. The release ships with a full cast of named, playable characters at the heads of these houses, from the Wyvern King in his frozen hall to the immortal serpent queen and her sprawling brood of scheming children.
