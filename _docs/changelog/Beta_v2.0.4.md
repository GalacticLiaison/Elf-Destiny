# Beta_v2.0.4

NEW! Red Sister temples on the map
   - Every temple a Red Sister keeps is marked on the political map with her portrait. Click the marker to open her.
   - Only the Order sees the markers: Sisters, Matrons and the High Matriarch.
   - Markers appear the day a Sister takes a temple into her care and are refreshed every season.
   - Matronates now start with temples for their Sisters: up to two in each Matronate at every start date.

Religion
   - Red Sisters keep their ruler's temples through the Order's lease, and only that way. A Matter of Land no longer hands a Sister a temple of her own; where the realm has no temple the Order still offers to raise one, and the finished temple passes into her care.
   - Sisters who already hold a temple from an earlier version keep it.
   - The Order's reproach for a ruler whose Red Sister has no temple now says so: Red Sister Without a Temple.
   - Archdioceses pass to the Order instead of falling. A Christian see whose seat an elven Aeluran ruler holds, with half its counties Aeluran, becomes a Matronate under a raised Matron. A seat that converts early leaves the see standing until then.
   - Every year the Order claims the sees that qualify, including ones destroyed earlier, and puts every ruling Sister on the Order government.
