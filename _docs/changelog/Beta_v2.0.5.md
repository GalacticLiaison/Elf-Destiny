# Beta_v2.0.5

Religion
   - A Sister's robes now follow her tenet: black for the Dark Sisters, red for the Aeluran Sisters, whatever her race. A Sister sent to a ruler's court takes the ruler's rite, so a dark-elf ruler on the Aeluran Sisters tenet gets a dark-elf Sister in red, and a light-elf ruler on a Dark Sisters rite a light-elf Sister in black.

# Bugs/Balance
- Fixed newborns and children drawn with adult faces and bodies since Beta_v2.0.4: the age curves failed to load.
- Fixed the yearly claim of a qualifying archdiocese failing to settle its new Matron at the seat's court.
- Fixed Aeluran magi reinforcements since the 1.20 update: the Magistration event fired but no soldiers arrived, under every government. The Order's magi are now pledged while a ruler is at peace and assemble at the capital the first time she goes to war; from then on the Order refills that host as before. The pledge and the host's strength are listed under the Aeluran Advisor's Magistration task.
- The Aeluran Advisor no longer has to hold a temple for the Order to pledge magi, and the deliveries no longer stop when she works on another council task; any ruler with an Aeluran Advisor receives them.
- Temples leased to the Aeluran Advisor now feed the Order's magi: each adds 10 magi to every delivery and one more sub-regiment to the host's cap, on top of what titled Aeluran Order vassals owe by their contract. Temple buildings will raise this later.
- The Magistration task is now the Advisor's own contribution: deliveries grow by 1% per point of her Learning, and the ruler's Spark Wielder regiments gain 0.5% damage and toughness per point while she magistrates.
- A ruler who leaves the Aeluran faith loses the Order's host and its pledges.
- The two-year wait after an Aeluran Advisor takes her seat is gone: the first pledge comes with the next season. The Magistration task description now always states the host's status: what stands, what is pledged, what each delivery would bring and from where, or why nothing feeds it, and when the next delivery is due.
- The magi the Order has pledged appear in the Military window, under "Pledged by the Aeluran Order", as a Special Troops style entry with the pledged counts and where they come from, until the host assembles.
- Rulers with no Aeluran Order vassals no longer receive a token one-magus delivery; the message now says why nothing is owed.
- Aeluran Judiciary now raises vassal magi contributions (+75%), above Institutionalized Authority's +50%; it gave no bonus before.
- Every culture of elven heritage now knows Elven Adaptability, the innovation behind the extra tradition slots: the basic Dark Elf culture an expedition party can take had no history and so no innovations, which is why embracing it gave no slots. Existing saves are healed at the next new year.
- The expedition's "A Discovery of Cultural Importance" now names the Dark Elf traditions (The Old Ways, Night Mothers, The Black Crown) instead of "Error - Unknown tradition name", and those traditions are listed with the other elven traditions when adding or diverging a culture.
- The Drow and Dökkálfar cultures now have their name forms: a nomad realm of theirs read "The elf_culture_dokkalfar_collective_noun".
- The Familial Familiarity tradition exempts its culture from consanguinity rules again: since 1.20 the marriage, betrothal, concubinage and incest-secret checks hooked a trigger the game no longer calls, so the tradition did nothing for them.
- The Seduce and Courting schemes' taboo penalties (incest, sodomy, adultery) apply again; their checks named triggers 1.20 renamed, so they never fired.
- Demand Conversion, the armor and helmet portrait rules and the other overridden game triggers are brought up to the 1.20 versions (puppet actors, adventurer camps, military orders, military governors). Magi still never wear armor.
- The Drow and Dökkálfar cultures now choose a culture head; they lacked the head-determination pillar 1.20 requires.
- Elven Elective succession works again: its elector and candidate lists used the pre-1.20 syntax, which the game rejected at load, so no elven election had electors or candidates since the patch.
- The bloodline events (the orphan with a letter, the visiting tourney champion, the star-crossed pregnant elf) now bring the four Dark Elf houses: a Dark Elf ruler meets orphans, champions and lovers of Houses Drenmyre, Rynvela, Syldranis and Myrvessa, as Dark Elves of the matching culture, while light elf rulers keep the six light houses.
