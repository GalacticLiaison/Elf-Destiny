# Beta_v2.0.7

# Bugs/Balance
- Fixed a crash that hit on the same date every time in saves where a Red Sister's temple marker had gone stale (the Sister dead, replaced on her seat, or the lease ended).
- AI entrancement toned down: AI characters attempt to entrance half as often, wait twice as long between attempts (and ten years before trying the same target again), and fewer of them want to in the first place. Seducers, Enchantresses, the Ambitious and close family still lead the attempts.
- The Aeluran Order no longer sends an Advisor to rulers whose government has no council (steppe nomads, holy orders, landless rulers); she could never take her seat, and a new one was sent every quarter.
- A Matter of Land no longer tries to found a temple on a barony the ruler has lost; the Sister asks for a temple to be assigned instead.
- Under the Aeluran Order's government, councillors who do not meet the Order's gender rule are dismissed again; the effect went missing in 2.0.6.
- Grand Portal I and II: their monthly piety, dynasty prestige and lifestyle experience bonuses now apply to any holder of the building, since 1.20 no longer supports building bonuses limited to one faith.
- The Valerith bloodline's and the Warg trait's scheme modifiers apply again, and marriage acceptance for administrative realms uses its four rules again; all referenced names 1.20 removed.
- Battle of Wills: the Aeluran Order rank difference counts again; that part of the roll was always zero.
- Fixed a wide variety of minor bugs mentioned in error logs.
