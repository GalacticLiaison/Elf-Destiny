# Beta_v2.1.0

Updated for Crusader Kings III 1.21 "Silk & Silver". Saves from earlier versions of the mod will not carry over.

NEW! Elven trade goods
   - Four elven goods join the new markets: Lembas, Elven Crafts, Mythril and Mythril Elven Crafts.
   - Lembas is baked at a Lembas Bakery from Grain and Honey. Elven Crafts are made at an Elven Workshop. Mythril Elven Crafts are made from raw Mythril at a Mythril Forge. All three are built in a Guild district, or in the tribal economy district.
   - The Elven Workshop needs the Elven Craftsmanship tradition. The Bakery and the Forge need knowledge that has been lost, see below.
   - Everyone wants the finished goods, elves and humans alike. Twelve vanilla buildings gain an extra bonus when supplied with them:
   - Elven Crafts: High Street Market, Town Market, Castle Court, Great Hall and Reception Rooms give more trade income, prosperity, prestige and court grandeur.
   - Lembas: Castle Larder, Victualing Depots, the March, Temple Hostels and Merchant Lodging Houses give more food, supply limit, faster levy reinforcement and safer roads.
   - Mythril Elven Crafts: the Armorer makes stationed men-at-arms tougher, and the Royal Armory makes knights more effective.

NEW! Lost knowledge
   - Lembas Baking and Mythril Working are innovations no culture knows at the start of the game. They cannot be researched.
   - Both can be found on expeditions, alongside portal pieces and traditions, each with its own discovery event.
   - Once one elven culture knows an innovation, it slowly spreads to the elven cultures that accept it.

NEW! Mythril
   - Eight mythril veins lie hidden across the map at the sites known from the Europa Universalis V version of the mod. A vein is worked with a Mythril Mine.
   - The Divine Spark's blessing ritual can now offer a Blessing of Wealth, which reveals a new vein in one of your own holdings, most likely in mountains or hills. A follow-up event shows you where. It can be granted once per dynasty.

NEW! Districts
   - Woodland Grove Farms are now a building for the resource district of any forest or taiga province, for cultures that can farm the woods. They produce Grain and can be built alongside the vanilla grain buildings.
   - Rulers of the Aeluran Order can build the tribal districts and their buildings, and the Tribal Hall.
   - Ascended Tribal rulers keep the upper levels of the Tribal Hall.

NEW! Lormelis raiders
   - The Lormelis bloodline lives up to its name as the sea raiders of Atlantis: anyone who carries it can raid under any government, over land and across the sea.
   - A dynasty whose head carries the Lormelis bloodline can unlock the Pillage dynasty legacy (requires Northern Lords).

# Bugs/Balance
- Royal bloodline looks now always win over the Dark Elf colours: a Myrvessa keeps her red hair, a Valerith her silver hair, a Thundarael her pale skin, and Serelion and Lormelis keep their eyes, even when they become or descend from a converted Dark Elf. Thanks to Generalissimo.
- Creating a branched rite of an elven faith no longer costs 30 divergence for a "Hostility Group - Abrahamic" it never had, which left room for only one or two changes before the rite risked being branded heretical.
- Daughters returning from their schooling with the Aeluran Order are no longer added to a court they are already in.
- The elven pregnancy pulse no longer picks partners who cannot have children.
- Fixed a wide variety of minor bugs mentioned in error logs.
