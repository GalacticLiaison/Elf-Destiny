# Beta_v2.0.1 - Fresh Ink

NEW! Tattoos, and a lot of them
   - 105 tattoo designs across 15 slots, including new cheek and leg slots
   - Spider webs, serpents, smoke, elven script, stars, flowers and thorns. Something for every mood, most of them brooding.
   - Old tattoos finally have real names. Farewell, "Forehead Pattern 1".
   - Eye shadows are grouped at the top of the eye list, with Heavy Kohl and Streaked Kohl right behind them

NEW! Pick Your Ink
   - Tattoo ink color picker in the Ruler Designer's Tattoo Colour tab
   - One color for every tattoo, or a different color for each slot

NEW! Ruler Designer Updates
   - The Tattoo Face and Tattoo Body tabs are ordered by body area, with a heading above each slot
   - Rotate the body to check your back, arm and side tattoos
   - Body shape is now selectable (apple, hourglass, pear, rectangle, triangle), and body and bust shapes show readable names

NEW! Barbershop Updates
   - Tattoo Ink Color Picker, so existing characters can change their ink mid-campaign
   - Nudity selector right above the color pickers. For inspecting tattoos. Obviously.
   - The clothing and tattoo color pickers fold away when you're not using them

NEW! Bloodline Ink
   - Serpent tattoos (Winding Scales, Scaled Collar, Coiling Scales) are only chosen by drow in the court of a Syldranis bloodline ruler
   - Spidersilk Net is only chosen in the court of a Myrvessa bloodline ruler
   - Widow's Hose spider-web leggings are only chosen by dark elf women in the court of a Myrvessa bloodline ruler

# Bugs/Balance
- Fixed two chest tattoos whose textures failed to load
- Random tattoo rates outside those courts are unchanged
