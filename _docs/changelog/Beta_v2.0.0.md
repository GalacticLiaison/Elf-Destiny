# Beta_v2.0.0 - The Dark Elf Update

The Dark Elf Beta has completed and now is fully live!

NEW! Dark Elves!
   - Every elf tier now has a dark counterpart, all the way up the ladder
   - Purple, grey, and blue skin looks with matching hair options
   - The dark strain is inheritable. Watch the cradle.
   - updates to Ruler Designer!

NEW! The Spark Judges You
   - The expedition transformation has been reworked. The Divine Spark now weighs your whole life, every kindness and every cruelty, and hands down a verdict of Light or Shadow.
   - You may defy the verdict. The Spark remembers, and so does your stress bar.
   - Transformed rulers choose the skin and hair color for their entire party. Coordinate your outfits.

NEW! Dark Elf Realms, playable in 867, 1066, AND 1178!
   - Svartalfaheim, where Malric the Wyvern King rules House Drenmyre. It is nearly black on the map. That is on purpose.
   - Valusia, ruled by the ancient Queen Zynvara Syldranis. Five husbands, ten children, zero regrets.
   - Menzoberranzan, held by the Myrvessa triplets. Blood red on the map, for tasteful reasons.
   - Hrafnvir Rynvela broods over his corner of Iceland, as one does.

NEW! Four Dark Bloodline Traits!
   - Drenmyre black sclera, Rynvela black lips, Myrvessa spider-red hair, Syldranis emerald eyes

NEW! Dokkalfar and Drow cultures!
   - Two full name lists
   - NEW! traditions with icons and tattoos. Night Mothers, The Black Crown, and The Old Ways.

# Bugs/Balance
- Humans walking around with elf ethnicities are now converted into actual elves at game start. No more posers.
- Dark elf bloodline traits are available in the Ruler Designer and the debug trait picker, along with unconventional and exotic beauty tiers. Enjoy, cheaters.
- The Elf Finder now handles dark elves, purple glow included
