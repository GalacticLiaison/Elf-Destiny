# Beta_v2.0.3

NEW! Growing Matronates
   - Petition for a Matronate: an Aeluran ruler whose realm holds three or more Aeluran counties outside any Matronate can ask the High Matriarch to found one around their capital. A Sister of their court is raised to Matron, or the Order sends one.
   - Matronates grow with their flock. Counties conquered, granted or converted next to a Matronate join it.
   - New decision for Matrons, Extend the Matronate, gathers in the Aeluran counties bordering it.
   - The AI High Matriarch now founds Matronates where elven realms are without one.
   - When the capital of an archdiocese turns Aeluran under an elven ruler, it becomes a Matronate.
   - Any landed Sister can be raised to Matron and given a Matronate, and moves to the Aeluran Order government.

NEW! The Matronate of Svartalfaheim
   - Queen Vaelmyra of House Drenmyre is now a Matron, holding the new Dökkálfar Matronate of Svartalfaheim, which covers Lapland, Lofoten and Iceland.

NEW! The Sisters are clergy
   - Aeluran Sisters who rule now count as clergy. A Sister ruler can create and edit rites herself, and Sisters get the clergy events and interactions of By God Alone in their own faith's terms.
   - Sisters can demand vows of chastity.
   - Sisters keep their own succession, can still marry, and keep their realm priest.
   - Every Sister, Matron and Matriarch who rules is on the Aeluran Order government.
   - Matrons start with a few Sisters of their rite at court. Dark Matrons get Dark Sisters.

Religion
   - The Aeluran Weavers now start reformed, and every faith can hold four core tenets
   - Aeluran rulers can make their Red Sister their realm priest puppet, which lets them reform the faith and create or edit rites
   - The High Matriarch is now playable without By God Alone
   - Red Sister Indoctrination now spreads your rite: a Void Red Sister converts counties to the Void, and counties of your faith but another rite can be converted
   - Moving the Grand Portal now moves its holy site and relics with it

# Bugs/Balance
- Fixed Red Sisters losing the temples they were given, and a new Red Sister arriving every month or so
- The new temple the Order pays for is only handed over once it is built
- A player Matron's death is no longer a game over: you continue as the Sister who takes up her Matronate
- Elf and High Elf parents now have children of either kind, instead of always High Elves. Two elves never have a half-elf child.
- Fixed Brittany starting Catholic, and parts of Ireland and Sicily turning Catholic too early
- Hrafnvir no longer starts as a Catholic bishop in 1178
- Hid the Legatine Mission and Renounce candidacy decisions for the Aeluran Order
- Wyrm's Den uses the right succession for its government
- Feyswood now sits within its de jure empire
- Fixed a large number of background errors (regency, Swing the Scales, births, portraits, court chaplain)
