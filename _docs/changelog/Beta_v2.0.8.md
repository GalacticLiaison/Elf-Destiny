# Beta_v2.0.8

NEW! Community elves
   - More than thirty elves designed by the community in the Discord's elf-tinder and dark-elf-tinder channels join the world, in all three start dates, each with their creator's looks and their own outfit.
   - Most belong to the four Dark Elf houses: the courts of Malric, Zynvara, Triel, Faeryl and Hrafnvir gain new family and courtiers. A few join the other elven courts.
   - Two of them rule: Völundr, Malric's brother, is Count of East Kiilt, and Vilasanda is Countess of Boubo under Duchess Triel.
   - Four lead their own adventurer bands: Yeverinda Darkstar, Zaritha Fellfrond, Nizzre Lolthryn and Dinin Darkmire.
   - Seven new dynasties and three cadet houses, with the coats of arms their creators made.
   - Queen Zynvara's son is now Gromph, and she has a fifth husband, Ryvenaf.
   - The new characters appear in a new game.

# Bugs/Balance
- The religion map mode no longer stutters, and its "Display Religious Heads" toggle works: one of the mod's map files had not followed the 1.20.1 update.
- The guests with no name and no culture at the courts of Queen Zynvara and Duchess Triel are gone, and Svartalfaheim, Menzoberranzan, Valusia and Kamula belong to their de jure realms again (new games).
- Joining the Aeluran faith through the story no longer costs your household stress, piety and a five-year convert penalty, which 1.20 added to family conversions. Julia Melwood converts with you wherever she is.
- Julia Melwood follows the faith of her homeland: the undivided Church before the Great Schism, the Catholic Church after it.
- No historical elf appears twice in one start date any more: about twenty copies meant for another start date were also alive.
- The adventurer bands of Belanor, Aubrey, Heldrin and Myrddin have their leaders in the 1066 start.
- Family dates that could not be true are fixed across the bloodline houses: children born after a parent's death, and parents too young for their children.
- The Matron's Hall shows a placeholder in its building slot: its buildings are still in development.
