## Report
**vadersfaust1313** · *2025-11-03*
It seems that the mod is overriding the special buildings of the Wanua Government type. If you start in SEA you can only build the “normal” tribal buildings but not the special buildings for Wanua which makes it impossible to play a Wanua Government since you need the special buildings to generate Barter Goods that you need for MAA.
